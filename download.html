First steps

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Ratica onto Applications.
  2. Open Ratica from Applications. It is signed and notarized by Apple.
  3. Click Download and set up: Ratica downloads its engine and AI model once (about 5–6 GB).
  4. Choose a PDF, pick a language and press Translate.
